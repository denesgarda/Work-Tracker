How the rate is worked out

Deposits arrive weeks after the work that earned them, and they arrive in lumps covering several jobs at once. Rather than guess which hours a deposit belongs to, this adds up money and hours over the same window and divides. Over a long enough window the lag washes out — which is why weekly views show hours and income but no rate, and why 90 days is the window worth trusting.
